Music tab — the new centerpiece

This is where all the enriched metadata lives as analysis, not just display.
TASTE PROFILE: What wins in this league?
Scatter plot
X = popularity, Y = score
Colour = submitter
Trendline + confidence band
Findings:
• Popularity penalty: −0.04 pts per popularity point
• Sweet spot: popularity 30–50 (avg +1.8)
• Era sweet spot: 1990–2005 (avg +1.4)
• Duration: no significant effect (p > 0.3)
Strategy tip: Submit something from the late 90s that peaked at #40 and you'll outscore a current #1 hit by ~3 points on average.

New features enabled by music metadata (all options)

  • Popularity × score correlation (scatter plot + trend)
  • Era × score bias analysis (decade bars)
  • Duration analysis (does length help/hurt?)
  • Player "music personality" labels (Archaeologist, Curator, Populist)
  • Per-player era fingerprint (their decade distribution)
  • "Deepest cut to score" superlative
  • "Biggest hit to bomb" superlative
  • "Oldest/newest song" superlatives
  • Wikipedia "Liner Notes" — facts inline on songs
  • "Strategy tip" — the optimal profile to win
  • Taste clusters that correlate with era/popularity preference
  • "If Greggo shifted eras, his projection changes by X"
  • Total listening time stat
  • Duration column in song table
  • Year column + decade filter
  • Popularity column + obscurity badge
