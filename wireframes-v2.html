Six tabs, each answering one question. Green-flagged numbers are real — computed from your export, including 65 of 68 release years pulled from MusicBrainz (no API key). v1 is still at wireframes-final.html.

What changed from v1, and why
1. Two Carolines. Your roster has Caroline (never voted, last, −28) and Caroline Cone (leader, +44). v1's copy mixed them up repeatedly. Prose now always uses full display names, and a clarifier appears wherever both feature.
2. Archetypes kept — now blended from submissions (weight 2) and upvotes (weight 1), which turned out to be more interesting than either alone.
3. Downvote targeting added. Every voter spends exactly 6 downvote points a round, so the question is where they aim, not whether. Arch-nemesis framing kept.
4. Rounds folded into Play-by-Play so per-round results live in one place.
5. Overview teases, never duplicates — one item per category with a link, full lists in the destination tab.
6. Real round winners — and rounds 4, 5 and 6 were all topped on raw score by someone who forfeited. That is the story of your season.

How a chapter is assembled
Always
Winner, score, art · theme text · song and voter counts · standings after

Guard rails
One angle per player for the whole season, so Caroline's forfeits are told once as a pattern rather than six times.
Full display names always — Caroline and Caroline Cone are different people and prose must never shorten either.
No superlative claims before round 3.
Round-relative language, since a 10-point round can be a win or a mid-table finish depending on turnout.
Never conflate the best song with the winner. In rounds 4, 5 and 6 the top raw score was forfeited, so the trophy went to a lower-scoring song. The chapter names the credited winner and reports the forfeited score as the twist.
